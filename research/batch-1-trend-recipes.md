# Batch 1 — Copy-the-Trend Recipes (week of Oct 12, 2026)

Each recipe copies the format that is already going viral. Nothing here is a new concept: same structure,
same audio, same timing, made with AI instead of a camera.

**Ground rules that keep the channel alive**
- **Audio:** never download and re-upload a song. On TikTok, tap the sound on a trending video and use
  "Use this sound". On YouTube Shorts, open a Short that uses it and tap "Remix → Use sound". That way the
  audio is licensed and the trend algorithm links your video to the trend.
- **Copy the format, not the footage or the name.** Reposting someone's clip or using a real show's name and
  logo is what got Fruit Love Island's episodes removed.
- **Vary each upload.** Same trend is fine; the same video re-rendered 10 times is what YouTube demonetises.
- **Tick the AI/synthetic-content disclosure** on realistic AI videos.

## Recipe 1 — Ramalama costume-walk reveal (post daily until Oct 31)

**Audio:** "Ramalama (Bang Bang)" — Róisín Murphy (use the sound from a trending video)
**Length:** 12-15s · **Caption:** `did I win this trend? 🎃` · **Tags:** #ramalama #halloween #costume #fyp

| Time | Shot |
|------|------|
| 0-6s | Camera at ankle height in a dim hallway. Boots walk slowly toward lens, one step per beat, dragging a prop. |
| 6-7s | Beat drops → hard cut. |
| 7-13s | Camera tilts up: full costume, still walking toward lens, stops, stares into camera. |
| 13-15s | Hold the stare; loops cleanly back to the boots. |

**AI prompt (video):** `Vertical 9:16, cinematic horror lighting, camera at ankle height in a dark
concrete hallway, heavy black boots walking slowly toward camera dragging a rusty chain, flickering
fluorescent light, then camera tilts up revealing [COSTUME], figure stops and stares into lens.`

**Make 5 variants by swapping [COSTUME]** (generic, no trademarked characters):
1. a vampire in a red velvet cape
2. a scarecrow with a burlap face
3. a porcelain doll in a Victorian dress
4. a plague doctor with a beaked mask
5. a pumpkin-headed knight in rusted armour

## Recipe 2 — AI fruit reality-show episode (the Fruit Love Island format)

The format, copied: AI fruit characters, a dating-villa setting, confessional-style drama, numbered
episodes, cliffhanger ending. Use a generic title like **"Fruit Villa"**, never "Love Island" or its logo.

**Length:** 60-75s · **Title:** `Fruit Villa — Episode 1: The New Arrival 🍓` · **Tags:** #aivideo #fruit #drama

| Time | Shot |
|------|------|
| 0-3s | Hook: Strawberry gasps at the villa door. Caption: "SHE brought HIM?!" |
| 3-15s | Confessional cut: Banana to camera, "I came here for love… not for drama." (smash cut) "Okay, a little drama." |
| 15-35s | Pool scene: new arrival, Pineapple, walks in slow-mo. Everyone stares. Mango drops her drink. |
| 35-55s | Fire-pit: host Coconut announces "Tonight, one of you is going home… single." |
| 55-65s | Pineapple picks… Strawberry's partner, Kiwi. Strawberry's face. |
| 65-70s | Cliffhanger card: "Episode 2 tomorrow 👀" |

**AI prompt style:** `Vertical 9:16, Pixar-style 3D, anthropomorphic [FRUIT] with expressive face wearing
[OUTFIT], luxury villa at golden hour, reality-TV camera angle, dramatic reaction.`
Generate each shot separately, voice with AI voices, add burned-in captions.
