# Outreach Scripts (English + Español)

Rule: lead with something specific and free. Never "I couldn't help but notice."
Replace everything in [brackets].

---

## 1. Walk-in (best channel, by far)

Go mid-afternoon (2–4pm), when it's slow. Never during the lunch rush.

**English**
> "Hi! Is the owner around? ... Hey, I'm [name], I live here in Doral. I help local businesses with their Instagram and websites. I love this place, and I noticed your Instagram hasn't posted since [month]. I actually made three sample posts for you, no charge. Can I show you real quick? It's 30 seconds."

*(Show the 3 posts on your phone.)*

> "I can do this every month: 12 posts in English and Spanish, you just approve them by text. It's $250 a month, and I handle everything. Want to try it for a month?"

**Español**
> "¡Hola! ¿Está el dueño? ... Mucho gusto, soy [nombre], vivo aquí en Doral. Ayudo a negocios locales con su Instagram y su página web. Me encanta este lugar, y vi que su Instagram no publica desde [mes]. Le hice tres posts de muestra, gratis. ¿Se los enseño rapidito? Son 30 segundos."

> "Puedo hacer esto todos los meses: 12 posts en inglés y español, usted solo los aprueba por mensaje. Son $250 al mes y yo me encargo de todo. ¿Lo probamos un mes?"

If the owner isn't there: leave a card or a printed page of the 3 posts with your number, and ask when they'll be in.

---

## 2. Instagram DM

**English**
> Hi [business name]! I'm [name], I'm local here in Doral. Huge fan of your [specific item]. I made 3 sample posts for your page, free, just to show what it could look like. Want me to send them over?

**Español**
> ¡Hola [negocio]! Soy [nombre], de aquí de Doral. Me encanta su [producto específico]. Les hice 3 posts de muestra para su página, gratis, para que vean cómo podría verse. ¿Se los mando?

Only send the posts after they say yes. People open replies; they ignore unsolicited attachments.

---

## 3. Website pitch (for businesses with no site or a bad one)

**English**
> "When someone Googles you, there's [no website / an old site that's hard to use on a phone]. I built a sample of what yours could look like. Here's the link: [demo link]. One-page site, English and Spanish, ready in 72 hours. It's $400, or free if you sign up for monthly social media."

**Español**
> "Cuando alguien lo busca en Google, [no aparece página web / aparece una página vieja que no se ve bien en el celular]. Le hice una muestra de cómo se vería la suya: [link]. Página de una sección, en inglés y español, lista en 72 horas. Son $400, o gratis si toma el plan mensual de redes sociales."

---

## 4. Follow-up (3–4 days later, if no answer)

**English**
> Hey [name], just following up on the posts I showed you. Happy to do a first month and you can decide after. No pressure either way!

**Español**
> ¡Hola [nombre]! Le escribo por los posts que le enseñé. Podemos hacer un primer mes y usted decide después. ¡Sin compromiso!

One follow-up only. Then move on.

---

## 5. Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "How much?" / "¿Cuánto cuesta?" | "$250 a month for 12 posts, everything included. The website is $400, or free with the monthly plan." |
| "I'll think about it." / "Lo voy a pensar." | "Of course. How about I do the first two weeks of posts, and if you like them, we continue?" *(Only if you're comfortable doing 2 weeks on spec. Cap it there.)* |
| "My nephew does that." / "Mi sobrino me ayuda con eso." | "That's great. When did he last post? If he's busy, I can take it off his plate, and he can still approve everything." |
| "I don't need Instagram." / "No necesito Instagram." | "Most people check Instagram or Google before they visit somewhere new. Right now they're seeing [last post from March]. That's the first impression." |
| "Too expensive." / "Está muy caro." | "I get it. I can do 8 posts a month for $175 to start." *(Never go lower than that.)* |
| "Can you also do ads / a logo / an app?" | "Let's start with posts and see the results first. We can add more later." *(Protect your 80/20.)* |

---

## 6. Closing checklist (when they say yes)

1. Send the one-page agreement (`client-agreement.md`).
2. Send a Stripe or Square invoice for the first month, upfront.
3. Ask for: logo, 10–20 photos from their phone, their best-sellers, hours, and access to their Instagram (or added as a collaborator in Meta Business Suite).
4. Deliver the first batch within 5 days. Speed wins the referral.
