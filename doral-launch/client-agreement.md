# Service Agreement

*Simple one-page agreement. Not legal advice. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian should sign alongside you.*

**Between:** [Your Name / Business Name] ("Provider") and [Client Business Name] ("Client")
**Date:** [date]

## 1. Services
☐ **Social Media Plan:** [12] posts per month on [Instagram / Facebook], bilingual (English and Spanish), designed, written, and scheduled by Provider. Client approves each batch before it posts.
☐ **Website:** one-page bilingual website, delivered within [72 hours / 5 business days] of receiving Client's photos and information.
☐ **Website Care:** hosting, small text/photo updates (up to [2] per month), and keeping the site online.

## 2. Price and Payment
- Social Media Plan: **$[250] per month**, paid in advance on the [1st] of each month.
- Website: **$[400]**, with 50% due before work starts and 50% on delivery. (Waived if Client is on the Social Media Plan for at least 3 months.)
- Website Care: **$[25] per month**.
- Payments by [Stripe / Square / Zelle] invoice. Work pauses if a payment is more than 7 days late.

## 3. Term
- Social Media Plan: **3-month minimum**, then month-to-month. Either party can cancel with 14 days' written notice (text or email is fine).

## 4. What's Included and What Isn't
- Includes [2] rounds of revisions per batch of posts or per website.
- Not included unless agreed in writing: paid ads, logo design, photography/video shoots, replying to comments/DMs, extra pages.

## 5. Client Provides
- Logo, photos, menu/service info, and account access (or collaborator access) within 5 days of signing.
- Client confirms they have the rights to any photos, logos, or content they provide.

## 6. Ownership
- Once paid in full, Client owns the website content and all posts made for them.
- The domain name is registered **in Client's name**, and Client pays its yearly fee (about $10–15).
- Provider may show the work in their portfolio unless Client says no in writing.

## 7. No Guarantees
Provider will do quality work on time but does not guarantee specific followers, sales, or search rankings.
