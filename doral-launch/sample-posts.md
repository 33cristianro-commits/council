# Sample Posts: Café Tepuy (demo client)

Post these on **your own portfolio Instagram** as "sample work", so prospects can see what you do.
Make each graphic in Canva: pick one template, change the photo and the text overlay. Same fonts and colors every time.
Use free stock food photos from Canva's library for the demo; real clients' posts use their own photos.

Colors: terracotta `#C4532B`, deep green `#2F5D50`, cream `#FBF6EF`. Fonts: Fraunces (headlines), Inter (small text).
