# Doral Launch Kit

Everything you need to land your first client. Total cost: $0.

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `demo-site/index.html` | Bilingual demo website (fictional "Café Tepuy"). Your portfolio piece. |
| `sample-posts.md` | 6 ready-to-make sample posts for your portfolio Instagram |
| `prompts.md` | The AI prompts that do 80% of the work for every client |
| `scripts.md` | Walk-in, DM, and follow-up scripts in English and Spanish, plus objection replies |
| `client-agreement.md` | One-page agreement to sign when someone says yes |
| `prospect-tracker.csv` | Import into Google Sheets (File → Import) to track prospects |

## Your first 7 days

**Day 1: Set up (1 hour)**
- [ ] Open `demo-site/index.html` and replace `[Your Name]` (it appears 3 times).
- [ ] Put it online: go to **app.netlify.com/drop** and drag the `demo-site` folder in. You get a free link in seconds. Save it, because it goes in every pitch.
- [ ] Create a business Instagram, e.g. `@[yourname].studio`. Bio: *Bilingual social media & websites for Doral businesses 🇺🇸🇻🇪 | DM for a free sample*.
- [ ] Import `prospect-tracker.csv` into Google Sheets.

**Day 2: Portfolio (1–2 hours)**
- [ ] Make the 6 posts in `sample-posts.md` in Canva and post them to your Instagram.
- [ ] Add the demo site link to your Instagram bio.

**Day 3: Build the list (1 hour)**
- [ ] Google Maps → "restaurants Doral", "barbershop Doral", "nail salon Doral", "auto detailing Doral".
- [ ] Add 20 businesses with **4+ stars but a weak online presence** (no site, an outdated site, or an Instagram that went quiet).
- [ ] Best hunting grounds: the strip plazas along NW 41st St, NW 36th St, and around Downtown Doral / CityPlace.

**Days 4–7: Pitch (1 hour a day)**
- [ ] Each evening, make 3 free sample posts for 3–5 prospects using **Prompt C** (about 15 minutes each).
- [ ] Next afternoon, walk in (2–4pm) and use the walk-in script. DM the ones you can't visit.
- [ ] Log everything in the tracker.

**Goal:** 20 businesses contacted and your first "yes" within 2–3 weeks.

## Prices (fixed, no custom work)
- **Social:** $250/month for 12 bilingual posts, 3-month minimum
- **Website:** $400, or free with the social plan
- **Bundle:** $350/month (site + social + hosting)

## House rules
1. Get paid upfront. Always.
2. No more than 15 minutes of free work per prospect.
3. Say no to extras (ads, logos, video shoots) until you have 5 clients.
4. Never post anything using a business's logo or photos until they've agreed.
5. No cold text messages. Walk in or DM.
